## New problems in this version

- **The accuracy claim is within noise.** "Improve mean final accuracy by 0.10 / 0.60 points" rests on MNIST gains of 0.04-0.18 against run SDs of 0.05-0.09, with one baseline seed. CIFAR gains of 0.4-0.8 sit inside SDs of 0.3-1.2. Say "comparable or slightly higher" until you have paired differences across seeds with an interval.
- **Pooled precision leads the abstract, intro and conclusion,** yet Section V-B says it isn't a confidence bound. Lead with "100% recall, 3 honest removals in 96 runs (two runs affected)". That is about 3 in 6,240 honest client-cases, but those cases aren't independent, so use a run-level bootstrap if you want an interval.
- **Candidate labels at the end of non-IID FR3 at 40% (62% and 72% of honest clients).** A label this widespread isn't informative. Say what it means operationally, or tighten the rule.
- **Publishing a known bug.** Section VII-D describes the Algorithm 4 line-13 availability bug and says fixing it needs a new evaluation. A reviewer will ask why you didn't fix it. Either fix it and rerun a subset, or show the logged decisions are unchanged under the corrected rule.
- **Notation clashes.** W is both warmup length and total work in Eq. 16. P is both the number of coordinates and the non-nominated peer set. C is both the nominated cohort and the Candidate state.
- **Captions got too terse.** "Fig. 3. MNIST client response scores" doesn't say what the axes are (asinh of the mean z-scores), that round 20 is shown, or that the runs are representative. Fig. 5 should note the baseline coverage.
- **Housekeeping.** Make an immutable archive (e.g. Zenodo DOI) before submitting, and use `\url` for the GitHub link so it doesn't break mid-word. The "research logs" row in Table VII reads like a lab artifact.
- **Held-out check.** You now admit defaults were set during development. A cheap fix is to run frozen parameters on unseen seeds, or on one new dataset such as Fashion-MNIST or a MedMNIST subset.
